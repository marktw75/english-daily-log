## Article

Whether he's jumping from helicopters or crossing whitewater rapids on tree trunks, risky exploits are all in a day's work for Bear Grylls. The British risk-taker's many achievements and survival skills have made him a household name around the world.

Bear was born Edward Michael Grylls on June 7, 1974, in Donaghadee, Northern Ireland. His sister, Lara, later gave him the now-famous nickname when he was a baby. She started calling him "Teddy Bear," which was shortened to just "Bear." From a young age, Grylls loved adventure and spent his days climbing and sailing with his dad or swimming in the ocean.

In 1996, though, a shocking accident nearly cut short his exciting life and prevented his future career. Grylls recovered and used the experience as the motivation he needed to overcome adversity and develop a never-give-up attitude. That year while skydiving, Grylls's parachute failed to open, plunging him nearly 5,000 meters to the ground. He survived, but his back was broken in three places. Though doctors told him he might never walk again, he beat the odds. Two years later he not only walked, but became one of the youngest people ever to climb Mt. Everest.

From 1994 to 1997, Bear served in a special branch of the British military, where he received intense training, including learning jungle survival skills. During those years, he learned to "improvise, adapt and overcome," qualities he has relied on for many of his adventures. Had he not been in the military, Grylls might not have been prepared for future events.

A few years later, the Discovery Channel approached Grylls with an idea for a TV series. They placed him in difficult situations around the globe and broadcast him using his survival skills.

That show, the wildly popular Man vs. Wild, which Grylls says led to more extreme-adventure broadcasts, became one of the world's most-watched reality TV shows. Additionally, the survival expert has penned over 80 books.

Over the years, Grylls has accomplished many amazing feats. But today, he states that the "hardest and best thing" he's ever done is his newest book, The Greatest Story Ever Told. The book tells the real-life story of Jesus through the eyes of those who knew him best.

In an interview, the author said, "That's been the greatest privilege of my life, to tell a story that's bigger than any of my own. Because this really is the greatest story!"

## Never Give Up

In an interview, Grylls was asked how he went from a broken back to climbing Mt. Everest in such a short period of time. Grylls responded by saying that he's not really special.

"I've learned a few skills over my life, but anyone can learn skills. But there is power in having a goal and a clear focus. I know that the key to everything is having a never-give-up attitude."

## Some of Bear Grylls's TV Shows

- Man vs. Wild: Grylls travels the world in search of challenges to his survival skills.
- Running Wild with Bear Grylls: The survival expert takes celebrities on wild outdoor adventures.
- Bear Grylls: Survival School: Brave children leave technology at home and learn survival skills in the wild.
- The Island with Bear Grylls: Grylls takes a group of people to a remote Pacific island and leaves them there with basic tools.
