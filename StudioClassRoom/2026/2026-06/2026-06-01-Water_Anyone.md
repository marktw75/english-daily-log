# Water Anyone?

Swapping water bottles for water bubbles

by Linda Ludwig

## Article

You're hot and thirsty, so you purchase a bottle of water to quench your thirst.

When you're done, you throw the bottle away. That bottle then very likely becomes part of the plastic that ends up being dumped into the oceans every year. If that trend continues, there could be more plastic than fish in the oceans in about 25 years. And it's not going anywhere fast. Research shows that plastic requires hundreds of years to decompose.

In 2013, Notpla Co-Founders developed something more sustainable to hold water - an edible water bubble, which was eventually called "Ooho Water." Completely tasteless and safe to consume, Ooho bubbles are made from seaweed, which is incredibly flexible and strong, and seawater salt (CaCl2). You can poke a hole in the outer layer and drink the water inside or pop the entire Ooho-water-ball into your mouth.

The London Marathon, among others, has begun offering runners Ooho as a substitute for water in plastic bottles. Ooho has also expanded into vending machines at a London sports center, reaching another milestone in the effort to make the world plastic-free.

Thirsty? Pop an Ooho into your mouth for a guilt-free drink!

## Key Point

- Ooho is an edible water container that is one solution to the growing problem of plastic waste caused by water bottles.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
quench (one's) thirst

`quench (one's) thirst` 的意思為「止渴」，與之搭配的主詞可以是人或飲料。受詞 `thirst` 表「口渴」，可引申表達「渴望」，之後加上 `for something` 表達對特定事物的渴望，通常指的是知識、名聲、成就等。而 `quench (one's) thirst for something` 就表示「滿足對……的渴望」。例如：The student studies day and night to quench his thirst for knowledge. 這位學生日以繼夜地學習以滿足他的求知欲。

### Tip 2
S + have/has + p.p. + ...

句型是現在完成式，可表達經驗、持續到現在的狀態以及發生在過去但與現在有關的動作，例如剛發生的事件。須注意現在完成所描述的事件不一定發生在最近，例如文中的倫敦馬拉松開始提供 Ooho 的時間是 2019 年，但仍與目前的狀態有關，即現在仍提供該飲品。例如：The discovery of antibiotics has changed the way we treat bacterial infections. 抗生素的發現改變了我們治療細菌感染的方式。
