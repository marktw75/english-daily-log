# Sonic the Hedgehog

Gotta go fast - through 35 years of history!

by Heather Lin

## Article

After speeding through more than three decades, Sonic the Hedgehog shows no sign of slowing down. From pixel beginnings to movies and everything in between, Sega's blue creature remains a part of popular culture.

In 1991, all was not well at Sega. Video-game competitor Nintendo's Super Mario was doing super business, and Sega was getting left behind. Designers Naoto Ohshima, Hirokazu Yasuhara and Yuji Naka were given one task: to create a character who could take on Super Mario. And thus, Sonic was born. Wearing sports shoes and a confident smile, Sonic represented 1990s cool.

On June 23, 1991, the first Sonic the Hedgehog game hit the shelves and was an overnight success. Naturally, several sequels followed. In each game, players help Sonic race through colorful zones, collect rings and battle at the end of every stage. Many levels also feature Sonic's mission to protect animals and nature from Dr. Robotnik's machines. These fast-paced challenges kept fans ready for the next adventure.

Decades later, Sonic keeps running - across screens, decades and imaginations. He's proof that the fastest heroes aren't just quick on their feet but timeless at heart.

## Key Point

- Sonic the Hedgehog's journey from a 1990s video game character to a lasting pop-culture icon shows why he remains popular decades later.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
take on

`take on` 為片語動詞，在體育或其他競爭語境中常表「與……較量」、「對抗」，語意接近 `compete against`。若受詞是人稱代名詞，要置於 `on` 的前方。

### Tip 2
adv.-V-ing vs. adv.-p.p.

複合形容詞可由「副詞 + 現在分詞」或「副詞 + 過去分詞」構成。現在分詞常表主動或進行，過去分詞常表被動或完成。
