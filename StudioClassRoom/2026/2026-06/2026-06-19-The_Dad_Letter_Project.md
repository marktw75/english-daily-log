## Article

Rosie's dad showed his love for her and helped her grow up - through letters.

Over the years, Rosie received thousands of handwritten letters from her father, Buz Ecker. The notes were filled with encouragement, gentle wisdom and fatherly advice. After her dad retired, he suddenly found himself with extra time but nothing meaningful to fill it with. Rosie began to ask herself if others might need the same support that she had received in the letters from her father.

She shared a message on social media about her dad. She said that he would love to write an encouraging letter to anyone who needed to hear from a dad. Instead of the few replies she expected, she quickly received over 1,000 requests, and the Dad Letter Project was created. Soon, other dads volunteered their time.

This operation connects people around the world who need support. Whether they're struggling, celebrating a milestone or simply feeling alone, volunteers will write personal, thoughtful letters to them that are heartfelt. Those caring messages can help make all the difference in the world.

## Quote

"I try to help with the problems they share. I'm honored they write to me and happy to write back." - Buz Ecker
