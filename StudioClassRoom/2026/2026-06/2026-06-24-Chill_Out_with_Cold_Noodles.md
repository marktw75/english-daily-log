## Article

One hot summer night, a friend was preparing noodles for dinner. But the thought of a bowl of hot, steaming noodles didn't sound good. Imagine my delight when the noodle dish turned out to be cold and tossed with my favorite sesame sauce!

It's reported that cold noodles have been enjoyed around the world since ancient times. Liangpi noodles, for instance, are from Shaanxi, China. The cold noodles, mixed with simple condiments, were a favorite of China's first emperor, Qin Shi Huang.

How about noodles for dessert? Faloodeh is a popular dessert in Iran. Cooked rice noodles are frozen with sugar and rosewater, resulting in a cool summer treat.

Bagged noodles are a cheap, convenient street food sold at stalls in Hong Kong. Cold noodles are placed in a small bag. Then shrimp and fresh vegetables are added, and everything is tossed in a sauce. The result: a ready-made meal to eat on the go.

When the heat and humidity are high, why not reach for a delicious bowl or bag of cold noodles? They're guaranteed to hit the spot!

## Pasta Fredda

A favorite light Italian summer dish is pasta fredda. Cold short pasta is tossed with fresh summer vegetables and tomatoes. Then everything is mixed in a light sauce and often served with Italian cheese on top.

Many people believe pasta started in Italy. But some research suggests that it came first from China. Italians love it so much, though, that it forms the basis of many of their meals.
