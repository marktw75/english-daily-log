# Dynamic Pricing

More and more prices are changing hour by hour

by Elizabeth Koroma

## Article

Dynamic pricing lets companies adjust costs using demand and data. But changes can raise concerns, especially when personal information affects prices, which can confuse customers, weaken trust and create problems.

Businesses have always set prices for goods and services based on a wide array of factors including how much demand they expect, the prices their competitors charge and the cost of production. But some of these factors can change rapidly, and today's technology allows businesses to change prices just as rapidly. This possibility has led many businesses to adopt a strategy called dynamic pricing, in which they adjust the prices of goods and services frequently. This model benefits businesses, and consumers may be able to use it to their advantage, but it also raises ethical and practical concerns.

With advances in computing, it is now feasible for businesses to adjust prices continually in response to ever-shifting factors. This helps companies to manage their inventories, lower prices when they need to use goods up and raise prices when they can to maximize profits.

The transportation industry has used forms of dynamic pricing for decades. Airlines offer lower prices on days when fewer people travel and raise prices as the travel date approaches. Uber's surge pricing, another form of dynamic pricing, increases the cost of rides at times when the most customers want to travel. But this also spurs drivers to accept jobs at these times, thus making more rides available. And now companies in other industries are using dynamic pricing as well.

When applied effectively, dynamic pricing can benefit consumers by providing lower prices during off-peak times. It also encourages businesses to compete by lowering their prices when they can.

However, dynamic pricing can create problems for customers by obscuring the actual cost of products. Dynamic pricing makes it difficult to compare prices that are offered by different retailers since those prices are always changing. Dynamic pricing can make customers feel taken advantage of because prices often rise just when they need a product most. Or if customers do not know what factors affect prices, they may conclude that the pricing system makes no sense. The resulting resentment can hurt companies by causing customers to feel less loyal, stop buying from them or leave negative reviews.

Dynamic pricing also raises ethical issues that surround fairness. Some dynamic pricing algorithms take into account personal information such as location or browsing history. This data is sometimes associated with factors like race and class, so many worry that poor people or those who are more vulnerable in other ways may be forced to pay more.

Dynamic pricing has both advantages and disadvantages, but it more than likely will become increasingly prevalent in the years ahead.

## Key Point

- Dynamic pricing lets businesses adjust prices in real time, which can improve efficiency and lower some costs, but it can also create confusion, resentment and fairness concerns for consumers.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
N, prep. + which (+ S) + V ...

這個句型是非限定關係子句的一種，用來為前面的名詞提供額外的、補充性的描述或背景資訊，並且必須用逗號與主句隔開。關係代名詞 `which` 用來指稱事物或概念，在關係子句中作為介系詞的受詞。介系詞如 `in`、`with`、`to`、`for`、`on` 等可放在 `which` 前，使句子語氣更正式。例如：The old library, in which I spent countless hours, is scheduled for renovation next month. 我在其中度過了無數時光的那座老圖書館，預計下個月將進行整修。

### Tip 2
When + p.p., S + V

以過去分詞開頭的分詞構句，完整原型通常是 `When + S + be + p.p., S + V`。當前半句的主詞與後半句主句相同時，可以省略重複的主詞與 `be` 動詞，留下 `When + p.p.` 的形式，語意為「當……被……時」。例如：When completed, the bridge will connect the two islands. 當橋梁完工時，它將連接兩座島嶼。
