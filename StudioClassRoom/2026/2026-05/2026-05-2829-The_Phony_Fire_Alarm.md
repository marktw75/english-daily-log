# The Phony Fire Alarm

The fire alarm was just a drill, or was it?

by David Morton

## Article

The fire alarm went off at exactly 10:23 a.m. Students evacuated, teachers shouting directions, students grabbing jackets and leaving behind notebooks and half-eaten bananas on desks. Ivy scanned the hall as they exited. It felt real. But something didn't sit right.

After 20 minutes outside, the all clear was given. Back in the classroom, whispers flew: It wasn't a drill, but there was no smoke. No fire trucks. No announcement.

By lunch, the real mystery hit: The drawer of confiscated phones in the front office was empty. Completely.

"I'm telling you," Dean said, popping open his soda, "this wasn't a joke. It was a cover job."

"Someone used the chaos to steal the phones," Ivy agreed. "But how did they trigger the alarm without pulling it?"

"The system's wired to the main server. You could maybe trigger it with remote access, but that would take serious skill."

Ivy raised an eyebrow. "Anyone you know have this kind of competence?"

"Well ..." Dean said finally. "Last week I was in the computer lab during study hall. Someone was testing SQL injections on the control panel of the school's website."

Ivy blinked. "Is that some kind of medical term?"

Dean grinned. "No. SQL is a database language, and ..." He paused, seeing the look on her face. "Let's just say it's a spicy way to log in without properly logging in."

"Who was doing it?" Ivy inquired.

Dean hesitated. "It's Caleb Vu."

Ivy and Dean rushed to the computer lab and found that Caleb had signed into Computer #3 just before everyone had evacuated.

Dean dug into the browser's raw HTTP request history and struck gold: one administration login, time-stamped 10:22 a.m.

"Definitely him," Dean grumbled, rolling his eyes. "Didn't even encode the data ... guess he's an amateur after all."

They found Caleb in the library, hurriedly typing in an attempt to cover his tracks.

"If you delete the whole log file," Dean said, "it'll raise more eyebrows than that messy injection you sent."

Caleb's eyes went wide as Ivy and Dean sat down on either side of him.

"Why'd you do it?" Ivy asked gently. "You could've hurt someone."

"I just needed a few minutes!" Caleb whispered. "My phone got taken this morning. I sent a message I didn't mean to send. I thought I was texting Ryan, but I sent it to Melody. I had to unsend it before she saw!"

Dean blinked. "Wait. This was about a crush?"

Feeling miserable, Caleb nodded. "I know it was dumb. I was frantic."

Ivy sighed. "You're lucky no one got hurt."

"Did she see it?" Dean asked, grinning.

Caleb held up his phone. "She posted a GIF of a confused rabbit ... five minutes before the fire alarm."

Dean winced. "Oof. Too slow. But hey, it might not be too late to turn yourself in before you get expelled for life."

## Key Point

- Dean and Ivy used a digital trail to solve a fake fire alarm case and discovered that Caleb caused it to recover his confiscated phone and unsend an embarrassing message.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
S + V, N1 + V-ing, N2 + V-ing and N3 + V-ing

這種結構用來在主句後補充多個同時發生的動作或狀態，讓畫面感更強。後面的 `V-ing` 片語通常用來描寫周遭人物或事物當下的動作。例如：The audience stood up, hands clapping, voices shouting and banners waving high. 觀眾站了起來，雙手鼓掌、聲音呼喊、旗幟高高飄揚。

### Tip 2
Feeling + adj., S + V

這是分詞構句，用來表達主詞在某種情緒或狀態下做了某事。完整概念可理解成 `Because/As S felt ...`，但可省略成更精簡的形式。例如：Feeling overwhelmed by the workload, the employee decided to take a day off. 因為工作量壓得喘不過氣，這名員工決定請一天假。
