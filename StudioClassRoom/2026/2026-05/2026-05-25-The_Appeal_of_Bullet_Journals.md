# The Appeal of Bullet Journals

## Article

Imagine a journal that combines efficiency with creativity. That's what a bullet journal, or Bujo, does.

Bullet journaling is a flexible way to organize your life. To create your own Bujo, start with a blank or lined notebook and a pen. The first page will be your Index, where you list page numbers and corresponding information. Next comes the Future Log, a place to record your long-term goals. This is followed by Monthly Logs, where you note important deadlines and events for each month. Your Daily Log, which comes next, is where you write your everyday to-dos, reminders and notes. After that, you can add Collections pages for lists such as books to read, bills to pay and other things you want to keep track of.

One thing that makes Bujos stand out is their symbol system. The symbols allow you to quickly identify each entry.

Colored pens, stickers, craft tape and more can make your Bujo something you'll enjoy using every day. Some people fill their journals with artwork while others keep things simple with just a pen. It's all about your personal style. That's the magic of a bullet journal!

## Key Point

- Bullet journaling is a different way to organize your to-do lists, activities and plans for the future.

## Focus on Phrases

plan for 為……做計畫；打算

keep track of (someone or something) 掌握……的動向；記錄……

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
S + be + followed by + N

這個句型表示「A 之後接著是 B」或「A 之後緊接著出現 B」。主詞通常是先出現的事物，後面用 `followed by` 接續後來發生的內容。例如：The heavy rain was followed by a sudden drop in temperature. 大雨過後，氣溫突然下降。

### Tip 2
keep track of ...

`keep track of` 表示「追蹤、掌握、記錄……的狀況」，可用來描述持續注意人、事、物的進展或數量。相反說法是 `lose track of`，表示「跟不上、忘記、失去掌握」。例如：It's hard to keep track of all my old classmates. 要掌握我所有老同學的近況很不容易。
