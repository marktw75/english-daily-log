# The Next Pitch: How Robots Will Change Baseball

Baseball's strike zone is entering a new era

by Garrett Hart

## Article

Is it a ball or a strike? Umpires often come under fire for mistaking one for the other. But help is on the way!

Major League Baseball (MLB) plans to use the Automated Ball-Strike System, or ABS, during the 2026 season. Often called a "robot umpire," ABS reduces human error. Its cameras and tracking equipment determine whether a pitch is a ball or a strike. The home-plate umpire will make the initial call for each pitch. ABS comes into play when a player challenges the umpire's decision. It will quickly confirm whether a pitch travels through the strike zone. Each team starts a game with two challenges.

ABS uses twelve high-speed cameras to track each pitch and judge whether it crosses the strike zone. Some people welcome a reduction in arguments, but others fear it could make baseball less exciting. But maybe not yet. ABS is not perfect since it must be changed for every player batting depending on their height. So, there's still a margin for error. One thing's for sure: With ABS, there's a new umpire in town!

## Key Point

- As of the 2026 season, Major League Baseball (MLB) is using a tracking system to help make calls.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
S + V + whether-clause

用來表達「主詞是否……」的疑問或決定。`whether` 是連接詞，後面接子句。若主詞是人，常搭配動詞 `know`、`wonder`、`ask`、`decide` 等；若主詞是事物，常搭配動詞 `show`、`indicate`、`determine` 等。此外，也可用 `whether ... or not` 結構，強調兩種可能性。例如：I wonder whether she will come to the party. 我在猜她是否會來參加派對。The final exam will determine whether he passes the course or not. 期末考將決定他的這門課是不是會及格。
