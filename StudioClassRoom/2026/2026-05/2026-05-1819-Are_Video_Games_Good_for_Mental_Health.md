# Are Video Games Good for Mental Health?

Does sitting for hours with that controller help you or hurt you?

by Ruth Devlin

## Article

Lights flash, music plays and the controller shakes. Video games have become a part of modern life. Kids, teens and adults spend hours exploring virtual worlds, solving puzzles or battling enemies online. Does this engaging form of entertainment help or hurt mental health? It seems everyone has an opinion about gaming's risks and benefits.

Some say gaming improves one's focus and problem-solving skills as well as reduces stress. For many, gaming is a creative and social avenue that brings people together. It can give players a sense of accomplishment and control, which boosts one's mood and motivation. A recent study also found that 88 percent of adult gamers reported positive benefits, such as feeling more social or relaxed.

However, some studies are far from positive. They show the flip side. Some experts say that too much gaming replaces healthy activities like sleep, exercise and in-person social time. This can lead to gaming disorder. Research also links too much play to lower self-esteem, anxiety and poor sleep.

With billions playing worldwide, understanding how gaming shapes mental health remains important.

Bella: I don't get why people say video games are bad. When it comes to playing them, I forget about being stressed.

Lucas: I get that, but I've seen people take it too far. My cousin plays all night, misses meals and hardly sleeps. My aunt and uncle are having a fit.

Bella: Well, that's not the games' fault. It sounds like your cousin has a gaming problem.

Lucas: I agree. So that proves gaming can be addictive.

Bella: I don't think anyone would deny that. But gaming can improve focus and teamwork. Plus, I've made friends online I'd never have met otherwise.

Lucas: Yeah, but not everyone finds balance. Sometimes people get addicted. If that happens, their grades and mental health suffer. I've read about players who lose control when they lose.

Bella: I get mad when I lose, but then I remind myself it's just a game.

Lucas: Then you don't get too intense.

Bella: No. Games can teach problem-solving and patience.

Lucas: I guess it's like anything else. Too much is not good, but a little is fine.

Bella: Agreed. If we balance gaming with real life, it can be good for us.

Lucas: I hope my cousin finds that balance.

## Key Point

- Video games can support relaxation, social connection and focus, but too much gaming may hurt sleep, self-esteem and mental health.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
It seems + (that) + S + V

意思是「看起來好像……；似乎……」，用以描述說話者根據觀察、感覺或推測所作的判斷。`It` 是虛主詞，真正的主詞是後面的 `that` 子句，其中 `that` 可省略。若要更簡潔，也可改寫為 `S seems to + V`。例如：It seems that the new marketing strategy is working effectively. / The new marketing strategy seems to be working effectively. 看起來新的行銷策略正在有效地運作著。

### Tip 2
When it comes to + V-ing

用來表示「一談到……」，常用於引出某個主題或特定情況。後面接動名詞或名詞，而不是動詞原形，因為 `to` 在這裡是介系詞而非不定詞的用法。類似用語還有：`in terms of + V-ing/N`、`As for + N`。例如：When it comes to cooking Italian food, my mom is the best. 說到煮義大利菜，我媽媽是最厲害的。
