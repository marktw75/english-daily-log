# Tuesdays with Morrie: An Old Man, a Young Man, and Life's Greatest Lesson

The greatest lessons come not from success but from compassion and connection

summarized by Simon Stopps

## Article

Some books remind us what really matters in life. *Tuesdays with Morrie* is one of them. Written by Mitch Albom, it tells the true story of his friendship with his former college professor Morrie Schwartz.

After graduation, Mitch learns that Morrie is dying from amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). The destructive disease is slowly destroying Morrie's body but has left his mind clear as if the illness had never touched him. Wanting to reconnect, Mitch begins visiting Morrie just as he used to during college.

Their weekly Tuesday meetings become life lessons that go far beyond the classroom. Morrie shares his thoughts on work, love, family and death with honesty and humor. He teaches Mitch that success and money lack significance compared to kindness, relationships and living with purpose. Even as his body weakens, Morrie's spirit remains strong, reminding readers that true strength comes from within.

Through these conversations, Mitch starts to see how much of his own life has been spent chasing the wrong goals. He begins to rediscover compassion and gratitude. The book invites readers to reflect on their own priorities, to slow down and to spend more time caring for the people who truly matter.

As Morrie's health continues to decline, his lessons grow even deeper. He tells Mitch that death should not be feared but accepted as a part of life. When people learn to face their mortality, Morrie says, they also learn how to live more fully. Each Tuesday becomes a chance for Mitch to understand what it means to be human: to forgive, to give and to love without hesitation.

One of Morrie's most memorable ideas is that giving to others is the key to happiness. Focusing on what you can get is a trap. Only by sharing with others will you find happiness. Morrie's warmth and courage inspire Mitch to live with more empathy and to reconnect with his own values. These moments show that wisdom often comes not from power or success but from humility and reflection.

By the end of the book, Morrie's passing is both heartbreaking and hopeful. Mitch's life is forever changed. Additionally, readers are reminded that life's greatest lessons are not found in careers or possessions but in the simple act of loving others. Morrie's message continues to touch hearts, proving that even in death his voice still teaches people how to live.

## Key Point

- In *Tuesdays with Morrie*, Mitch reconnects with his dying professor Morrie, whose Tuesday lessons teach him that love, kindness and purpose matter more than success or wealth.
- As Morrie's condition worsens, he shares deeper wisdom about accepting death, giving to others and living with empathy, profoundly transforming Mitch's outlook on life.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
how much of + N + has been spent V-ing

這個句型表示「有多少……（到目前為止）已經花在做某事上」，常用在時間、金錢或精力方面。`has been spent` 是現在完成被動語態，說明到目前為止已被花費；`V-ing` 則指出花費的用途。例如：`How much of your time has been spent working on this project?` 你有多少時間已花在這個計畫上？若指金錢，也可以說：`How much of the budget has been spent buying new equipment?` 已有多少預算花在購買新設備上？

### Tip 2
One of + plural N + is that ...

這個結構表示「其中一個……是……」，常用來說明多個事物中的其中之一。`one of` 後面必須接複數名詞，而動詞則依主詞 `one` 的單數形式搭配 `is`。後面的 `that` 子句用來補充說明「這其中一個是什麼」。例如：`One of the reasons I love this city is that people here are always friendly.` 我喜歡這座城市的原因之一是這裡的人總是很友善。
