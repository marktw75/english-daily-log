--# Paradise in the Indian Ocean

The Seychelles offer a natural experience like no other

by Ruth Devlin

## Article

What could be better than waking up to blue-green waves gently rolling onto smooth white sand? Not to mention hearing palm trees swaying in a warm ocean breeze and seeing giant tortoises wandering by. That's life in the Seychelles, a delightful island nation in the Indian Ocean about 1,600 kilometers off the coast of Africa. This group of 115 tropical islands is known for its seaside beauty, unique wildlife and luxurious calm.

The Seychelles have much to offer visitors. Travelers can explore the capital city, Victoria, with its lively market and delicious Creole food. And no visit to Seychellois National Park would be complete without a mountain hike. Praslin Island offers another must-see, Vallée de Mai, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, where the rare coco de mer palm grows, producing the world's largest seed.

The Seychelles are also famous for beautiful beaches like Anse Source d'Argent on La Digue. It is often called one of the most photographed beaches in the world. The large curved rocks that dot the beach create a scene unlike any other.

Wildlife lovers are drawn to the islands for their biodiversity. The Seychelles are home to many unusual species, such as the black parrot and the Aldabra giant tortoise. The surrounding waters are also rich with marine life, making the islands a delightful destination for snorkeling and diving.

The islands' culture is reflected in their Creole music, dance and cuisine. Local dishes feature fresh seafood, tropical fruit and spices, while island festivals celebrate both traditional and modern island life.

However, even paradise faces challenges. The Seychelles are on the frontline of climate change. Rising sea levels threaten their beaches, while warming ocean temperatures harm coral reefs that protect the islands and attract tourists. Too much development also creates pressure on fragile ecosystems. To fight this, the Seychelles government has created large marine protected areas. They're doing all they can to promote ecotourism and renewable energy.

Despite these challenges, the Seychelles remain one of the most beautiful and peaceful destinations on Earth. Whether exploring lush jungles, diving or relaxing on soft sand, visitors discover that the islands are not just a place to see. They're a place to feel connected to nature.

## Key Point

- The Seychelles are famous for their beaches, wildlife and Creole culture, but they also face climate and development pressures and are working to protect their environment.

## Usage Tip

### Tip 1
no + N + would be complete without ...

這個句型表示「沒有……，某事就不算完整」或「……是不可或缺的」。常用來強調某個經驗、活動或事物中必備的元素。例如：No success would be possible without hard work. 沒有努力，就不可能有成功。

### Tip 2
S + do all S can (do) to + V

這個句型表示「某人盡其所能去做某事」。`can` 也可依時態改成 `could`，用來描述已經盡力而為的情況。例如：He did all he could to stay awake. 他已盡其所能讓自己保持清醒。
